1. Return Eligibility

Items may be returned or exchanged within 30 days of delivery, provided that:

  • The shoes are unworn and in their original condition.
  • The original shoe box and packaging are included.
  • All tags, labels, and accessories are intact.
  • The item is not visibly damaged, washed, altered, or customized.
  • Proof of purchase or order information can be provided.

Shoes that have been worn outdoors may not be eligible for return unless the return is due to a manufacturing defect or an incorrect item being shipped.

2. Reasons for Return

Wrong Size or Fit

If your shoes don't fit properly, you may request a size exchange, subject to availability.

If the requested size is unavailable, Nova Shoes may offer a refund or store credit.

Wrong Item Received

If you received an item different from what you ordered, contact us within 7 days of delivery. Nova Shoes will arrange the appropriate replacement or refund.

Damaged or Defective Item

If your shoes arrive damaged or have a manufacturing defect, contact us within 7 days of delivery and provide photographs showing the issue.

After inspection, Nova Shoes may provide a replacement, exchange, repair, or refund depending on the circumstances.

Change of Mind

Returns due to change of mind may be accepted within the 30-day return period, provided the item meets all return conditions.

Return shipping costs for change-of-mind returns may be deducted from the refund or paid by the customer.

3. Items That Cannot Be Returned

The following items are generally not eligible for return:

  • Worn or visibly used shoes, except for verified defects.
  • Customized or personalized products.
  • Shoes damaged through improper use or care.
  • Items without their original packaging.
  • Items returned after the 30-day return period.
  • Clearance or final-sale items, unless they are defective or the wrong item was shipped.

4. How to Request a Return

To request a return:

  1. Contact Nova Shoes Customer Support through our official support channel.
  2. Provide your order number and the reason for the return.
  3. Provide photographs if the item is damaged, defective, or incorrect.
  4. Wait for return instructions from our Customer Support team.
  5. Pack the item securely using its original packaging where possible.
  6. Ship the item using the return instructions provided by Nova Shoes.

Do not send an item back without receiving return instructions first.

5. Inspection Process

Once Nova Shoes receives the returned item, it will be inspected to determine whether it meets the return requirements.

The inspection process may take up to 5 business days after the returned item is received.

If the return is approved, Nova Shoes will process the applicable exchange, store credit, or refund.

If the return does not meet the eligibility requirements, the item may be returned to the customer.

6. Refunds

Approved refunds will be issued to the original payment method whenever possible.

Depending on the payment provider or financial institution, it may take several additional business days for the refund to appear in the customer's account.

Shipping fees are generally non-refundable unless the return is caused by an error on the part of Nova Shoes or the item is confirmed to be defective.

7. Exchanges

Size exchanges are subject to product availability.

Only one exchange request may be permitted per order unless otherwise approved by Nova Shoes Customer Support.

If the replacement size is unavailable, the customer may be offered a refund or store credit.

8. Return Shipping

For returns caused by:

  • Wrong item shipped by Nova Shoes
  • Confirmed manufacturing defect
  • Damaged item received

Nova Shoes will cover the applicable return shipping costs.

For returns caused by:

  • Incorrect size selected by the customer
  • Change of mind
  • Personal preference

The customer may be responsible for return shipping costs.

9. Promotional and Discounted Items

Items purchased using promotional discounts may be subject to additional return and exchange conditions.

Unless explicitly stated otherwise, discounted items remain eligible for return under the standard return policy.

Items marked "Final Sale" are not eligible for return or exchange except where required by applicable law.

10. Policy Changes

Nova Shoes reserves the right to update this Return & Exchange Policy when necessary. The policy applicable to an order will generally be the policy in effect at the time the order was placed.

For questions regarding a return or exchange, please contact Nova Shoes Customer Support.

Orders & Returns

1. How long do I have to return my shoes?
You can request a return or exchange within 30 days of delivery, provided the shoes meet our return conditions.
2. Can I return shoes that I have worn?
Generally, no. Shoes must be unworn and in their original condition. However, if the shoes have a manufacturing defect, contact Customer Support even if you have worn them.
3. Can I exchange my shoes for another size?
Yes. Size exchanges are available subject to stock availability.
4. What if my preferred size is no longer available?
If the replacement size is unavailable, Nova Shoes may offer a refund or store credit.
5. I received the wrong shoes. What should I do?
Contact Customer Support within 7 days of delivery. Include your order number and photographs of the shoes you received. We will assist with the replacement or refund process.
6. My shoes arrived damaged. What should I do?
Contact us within 7 days of delivery and provide photographs clearly showing the damage. Our team will review the issue and provide the appropriate resolution.
7. Do I need the original shoe box to return an item?
Yes. The original packaging should be included whenever possible. Returns without the original packaging may be rejected or subject to additional review.
8. How long does a refund take?
Once your return is approved, Nova Shoes will process the refund. The time it takes for the funds to appear in your account depends on your payment provider or bank.
9. Will my shipping fee be refunded?
Standard shipping fees are generally non-refundable. However, shipping costs may be refunded when the return is caused by an error on our part or a confirmed product defect.
10. Who pays for return shipping?
Nova Shoes covers return shipping for incorrect, damaged, or confirmed defective items. Customers may be responsible for shipping costs for size changes or change-of-mind returns.

Product & Ordering

11. How do I know which shoe size to order?
Check the size guide available on the product page before placing your order. If you're between sizes, contact Customer Support for assistance.
12. Can I change my order after placing it?
Order changes depend on whether your order has already been processed or shipped. Contact Customer Support as soon as possible if you need to make a change.
13. Can I cancel my order?
Cancellation requests should be submitted as soon as possible. Orders that have already been processed or shipped may no longer be eligible for cancellation.
14. Do you sell authentic products?
Yes. All products sold directly by Nova Shoes are authentic Nova Shoes products.
15. What happens if an item I want is out of stock?
You may check the product page for restock information or contact Customer Support to ask whether the item is expected to become available again.

Payments & Delivery

16. What payment methods do you accept?
Available payment methods are displayed during checkout and may vary depending on your location.
17. How can I track my order?
Once your order has been shipped, Nova Shoes will provide tracking information through your registered contact details.
18. What should I do if my order hasn't arrived?
First, check your tracking information. If the package appears delayed or the tracking information has not changed for an extended period, contact Customer Support with your order number.
19. Can I change my delivery address after placing an order?
Contact Customer Support immediately. Address changes may be possible before the order is shipped but cannot be guaranteed once the shipment has been dispatched.
20. How can I contact Nova Shoes Customer Support?
Contact us through the official Nova Shoes customer service channel listed on our website. Please include your order number when contacting us about an existing order.
